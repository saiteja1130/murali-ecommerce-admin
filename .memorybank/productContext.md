# Product Context (murali-ecommerce-admin)

## 1. Merchant Workflows
1. **Adding & Updating Products**: Upload primary and hover images, define price and originalPrice, assign sizes and colors, set tags and stock availability.
2. **Category Hierarchy Management**: Create top-level Departments (Women, Kids) and nest Subcategories under them.
3. **Inventory Control**: Monitor low-stock alerts, toggle out-of-stock badges.
4. **Order Tracking**: Update customer order delivery stages.
